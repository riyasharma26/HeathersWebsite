About Me

Hi, I'm Heather. Before operating my mobile pet care business full-time, I worked in a grooming salon where I gained valuable hands-on experience working with pets of all sizes and temperaments.

I take pride in the ability to adapt my care to each pet's individual needs by offering private dog walking, pet sitting, and occasionally grooming services. My goal is to help them feel comfortable, safe, and loved while you're away.

As both an animal lover and a people person, I believe that great pet care starts with good communication and by building professional relationships. I value the trust and responsibilities that pet parents can share with me.

I have experience caring for senior pets, puppies or kittens, and animals with special needs.

I am First Aid certified and fully insured.

I'm also a trained artist that specializes in pet portraits and can offer custom paintings of you or your loved one's pet.

Policies

Clear policies help keep things fair and stress-free for everyone.

Booking & Payment

  • Payment will be due at time of booking confirmation
  • Accepted: Venmo (preferred) and Zelle
  • Short notice (less than 3 days): +$5 add on
  • New clients: Schedule a Meet & Greet to get started

Cancellations

  • Emergencies handled case-by-case
  • Same day notice: non-refundable
  • Sitting services — less than 1 week notice: 50% refundable
  • Sitting services — more than 1 week notice: fully refundable
  • Walking & drop-in visits — less than 3 days notice: 50% refundable
  • Walking & drop-in visits — more than 3 days notice: fully refundable

Pet Requirements

  • Up-to-date vaccinations required
  • Flea & tick prevention must be current
  • All dogs will be on leash outdoors
  • Please be open and honest about your pet's personality and any health conditions
